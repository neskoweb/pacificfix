About Us

Hi, I’m Alex, the owner of Next Level Home Services. I started this business with one goal: to provide homeowners with reliable, high quality handyman services they can trust. With a degree in Electromechanical Engineering and years of hands on experience in residential and commercial maintenance, I bring a detail oriented, problem solving approach to every project. I believe in doing the job right the first time, communicating clearly, and treating every home with the same care and respect as if it were my own. Whether it’s a small repair, a new installation, or a larger home improvement project, you can expect honest recommendations, quality workmanship, and dependable service from start to finish. My goal is simple: to earn your trust and become the handyman you can rely on whenever you need help around your home.
